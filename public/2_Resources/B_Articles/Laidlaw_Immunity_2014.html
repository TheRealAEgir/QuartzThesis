Summary : This article used a model of influenza infected mouse with or without transient CD4 depletion and showed that CD4 depletion lead to decrease in antigen-specific CD103/CD69/CXCR3 TRM in the epithelial ariways TRM Localization. Helped CD8 offered better protection against re-challenge with higher recruitement in airways and higher granzyme B, but no difference in virus titers. CD4 were important in both TCIRC and TRM formation, with kinetics showing CD4 arrival in the tissue one day before CD8, and maximum effect of CD4 help in the first week following infection. IFNg KO CD4 T cells had showed similar results, suggesting a role for IFNg with CD8 not being the target because IFNgR KO CD8 T cells showed not differences. Finally, this could be due to higher TBET expression in the CD4 depleted conditions, with TBET OE resulting in more TCIRC and less TRM with less CD103/CXCR3 expression. TBET could bind to CD103 and compete with SMAD3. Partial KO showed similar results. TGFbR KO T cells had higher TBET with reduced TRM and CD103 expression. TRM Phenotype TRM Differenciation

Experiment : Use of H3N2 GP33-41 expressing influenza infection, that generate well known CD8+ T cells response in which specific T cells can be followed with tetramer staining, plus intravascular antibody injection to discern resident from circulating T cells. Results : antigen-specific CD8 T cells can be found in blood and lung, with TRM expressing CD69 and CD103 and CXCR3, with reduced Ly6C/CD62L/CD127/TBET/GZMB.

Experiment : Transient depletion of CD4 with CD4-depleting antibody prior or concurent to infection (CD4 repopulation from thymus after ~2 weeks following GK1.5 treatement) Results : There was modest CD103 loss at effector timepoint and no difference in antigen-specific T cells at memry timepoint in the lung parenchyma, but there was less antigen-speicifc CD103, CD69 and CXCR3 TRM. Experiment : Transfer of P14 Thy1.1 into mouse depleted/control Results : There was less P14 T cells at localised at the airway epithelium

Experiment : Model with B cell specific for egg lysosyme, to remove the effect of antibody immunity, and re-challenge of immunized mouse (CD4 depleted during infection/control) Results : Mouse with helped CD8 T cells had better protection against re-challenge and greater virus-specific T cells recruitement in the airways with higher granzyme B, but virus titer was lower in the unhelped condition.

Experiment : In order to determine which population was affected and caused the previous results, helped/unhelped immunized mouse + Transfer of helped/unhelped Tcirc and re-challenge Results : Any condition with unhelped T cells resulted in decrease in antigen-specific T cells in the lung airways, with additive effect in double unhelped condition, and so CD4 are important for both TRM and TCIRC formation.

Experiment : Kinetic experiment to determine when CD4 help is important. Kinetic of cell number in the airways Results : effect at 3 dpi, but not at 7 dpi; important in the first week. CD4 seem to enter the tissue about one day before the CD8, at 4 dpi.

Experiment : Bibliography said IFNg was important, so same experiment in WT/IFNg CD4 T cells with or without depletion Results : There was the same decrease in CD103+CD69+ antigen-specific T cells (not CD8 btw) in the IFNg group, or in the CD4 depleted group. There was no effect in mouse with IFNgR KO CD8 T cells, suggesting that CD8 T cells were not the target of CD4-derived IFNg.

Other Results : There was mor TBET expression in antigen-specific CD8 T cells in the CD4 depleted group

Experiment : OE of TBET in P14 T cells and transfer in 1 dpi infected mouse. Results : There was more TCIRC and less TRM, and antigen-specific expressed less CXCR3 and had lower CD103 upregulation (in vivo or after ex vivo TGFb treatment) without affecting CD69. Experiment : ChIP-qPCR of TBET Results : There was binding of TBET in ITGAE CD103 region. There was no defect in pSMAD signaling following TGFb treatement, suggesting TBET inhibit CD103 expression in transcriptional activation of the gene.

Experiment : Transfer of WT/TGFbRII KO P14 T cells in mouse and infection with GP33-41 epxressing influenza strain Results : There was no CD103 expression and reduced TRM number, and these cells expressed higher TBET.

Experiment : Transfert of TBET+/+ or TBET+/- in infected mouse Results : antigen-specific in helped/unhelped conditions had lower TCIRC and higher TRM, which expressed more CD103, CD69 and CXCR3.

Laidlaw, B. J., Zhang, N., Marshall, H. D., Staron, M. M., Guan, T., Hu, Y., Cauley, L. S., Craft, J., & Kaech, S. M. (2014). CD4+ T cell help guides formation of CD103+ lung-resident memory CD8+ T cells during influenza viral infection.

Abstract

Tissue-resident memory T (Trm) cells provide enhanced protection against infection at mucosal sites. Here we found that CD4(+) T cells are important for the formation of functional lung-resident CD8(+) T cells after influenza virus infection. In the absence of CD4(+) T cells, CD8(+) T cells displayed reduced expression of CD103 (Itgae), were mislocalized away from airway epithelia, and demonstrated an impaired ability to recruit CD8(+) T cells to the lung airways upon heterosubtypic challenge. CD4(+) T cell-derived interferon-γ was necessary for generating lung-resident CD103(+) CD8(+) Trm cells. Furthermore, expression of the transcription factor T-bet was increased in “unhelped” lung Trm cells, and a reduction in T-bet rescued CD103 expression in the absence of CD4(+) T cell help. Thus, CD4(+) T cell-dependent signals are important to limit expression of T-bet and allow for the development of CD103(+) CD8(+) Trm cells in the lung airways following respiratory infection. .
